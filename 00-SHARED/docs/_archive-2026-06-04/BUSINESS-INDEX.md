## Quick Facts (for pitches / presentations)

### The f(0) Achievement
- **Before:** 58% T0 startup cost, auto-compact at T10, 1-2 work cycles per session
- **After:** 8% T0 startup cost, 7+ work cycles per session, perpetual piston
- **Cost delta:** 3× cheaper per unit (Haiku-first routing)
- **Value delta:** 7× more work done per session
- **Combined:** **21× better value per dollar**

### The Market
- **Beachhead:** 60K investigators/journalists globally
- **Beachhead WTP:** $100-300/mo (Maltego is $999+/yr, lower quality)
- **Beachhead TAM:** $72M/yr addressable
- **Expansion:** 50K-100K knowledge workers (legal, finance, medical, compliance)
- **Long-term:** Enterprise memory middleware (when Mem0/Zep bloat becomes problem)

### The Competitive Position
- **vs. Mem0/Zep/Letta:** Crystallization law (memory gets cheaper, not more expensive). Prompt transformation (not augmentation). Forensic audit trail (not just memory).
- **vs. Maltego:** AI-native, cheaper, better memory, investigator-focused.
- **Moat:** HONEY compounds over time. Switching costs rise. Cost dynamics favor staying longer.

### The Profitability Path
- **Month 3:** $735 MRR (15 Pro + 3 Professional customers)
- **Month 6:** $3,475 MRR (50 Pro + 12 Professional)
- **Month 12:** $10,970 MRR (120 Pro + 30 Professional + 2 Enterprise)
- **Gross margins:** 83-87% (API cost is 5% of revenue at scale)
- **Breakeven:** ~150 Pro customers OR ~25 Professional customers

### Why Now (Why f(0) Triggers Growth)
- **Before:** "Perpetual piston" was theoretical claim. Could hit token wall.
- **After:** Committed filesystem changes. Measurable T0 reduction. Prototype runs 7+ cycles without compaction.
- **Shift:** From "ambitious architecture" → "proven system." This is when customers will pay.
