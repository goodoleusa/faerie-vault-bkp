# 🐝 Hive Plugin — Tutorial

> The point: **help you engage and annotate your thinking visually, spatially, and through intertextuality.**

This isn't a dashboard plugin. It's a **creative cognitive substrate**. AI work flows in as canonical truth, and you draw, annotate, cross-reference, and re-route freely on top — non-destructively, with every annotation looping back as steering input on the next AI turn.

---

## What problem does this solve?

Reading an AI-authored mission graph in Obsidian today gives you the data, but it doesn't give you a place to **think alongside the AI**. Vanilla Obsidian separates annotation (annotations, comments) from editing (wikilinks, embeds). You either modify the AI's note (breaks the forensic chain) or sit beside it and take separate notes (loses the connection).

[Latticework](https://www.matthewsiu.com/Latticework) (Siu & Matuschak, 2024) solved this for personal knowledge management:

> *"Latticework is a system designed to unify annotation with freeform text editing in the context of personal knowledge management tools."*

The hive plugin takes Latticework's three primitives — **text references, highlights, marginalia** — and extends them with faerie's bearing vocabulary so every reference carries a semantic direction. A highlight isn't just a colored span; it's a typed connection ("N — this unblocks me", "S — I deliver here", "E — parallel to this", "W — I'm questioning this baseline").

Combined with bidirectional MCP feedback, your reading becomes the next session's steering input. The AI proposes the substrate; you annotate; the AI reads your annotations and shifts course.

---

## The three modes of engagement

### 1. Visual engagement — compass overlay + typed graph

Open any manifest or charter. Run `faerie: compass overlay for current note`. A [Juggl](https://juggl.io/) view opens in a side pane showing the note's neighborhood as a typed colored graph:

| Edge color | Bearing | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 🔴 jasper | N | "This unblocks me / I depend on" |
| 🟢 emerald | S | "I deliver to / this is downstream" |
| 🟠 coral | E | "Parallel sister work" |
| 🟡 amber | W | "I'm re-validating this baseline" |

You see the compass DAG as a force-directed graph. Drag nodes. Filter by bearing (show only N-edges). Pin the queen 🐝 to center. The visual language is consistent with the chat-mvp's Mission Steer panel and the Excalidraw scenes — same colors, same shapes, same compass orientation everywhere.

### 2. Spatial engagement — canvas + auto-arrangement

Open today's daily folder (`00-SHARED/Daily/{date}/`). A `{date}-board.canvas` lives there, auto-generated by the daily mirror:

- **Charters** 📜 across the top as parchment scrolls
- **Manifests** 📌 as honeycomb hexes, clustered under their charter
- **Trail polylines** 🐾 connecting the day's waypoints in chronological order
- **Mission halos** 🌐 around clusters of manifests sharing a charter prefix

You can drag any card around. Add your own cards (free-text thoughts, questions, sketches via the Excalidraw plugin). Draw arrows between anything. **The canvas remembers your edits.** When the daily mirror regenerates the canvas, your free-play layer is preserved — only auto-generated cards whose underlying MD changed get refreshed.

This is the Kinopio model applied to forensic work: real data underneath, free thinking on top, both preserved forever.

### 3. Intertextual engagement — trail-refs + annotations (Latticework-extended)

Read a manifest. Spot a passage that connects to another note. Select it, hit `CMD+Shift+H`. A picker asks: which bearing?

- Pick **N** → "this unblocks my current reading" → jasper-tagged ref written
- Pick **S** → "this delivers into what I'm reading" → emerald-tagged
- Pick **E** → "parallel work" → coral-tagged
- Pick **W** → "this is the baseline I'm re-questioning" → amber-tagged

The reference embeds inline in your note (or any open note you choose as destination) as a colored MD link. `CMD+Hover+Click` peeks it in an adjacent pane without leaving your current spot — Latticework's signature interaction. `Shift+Click` collapses/expands the embedded reference.

The references are typed. The graph knows. The AI sees them.

## How does this build on Latticework?

Latticework's primitives are the foundation. The hive plugin extends each:

| Latticework primitive | Hive extension | Faerie semantics |
|---|---|---|
| Text reference (`CMD+Shift+H`) | Bearing-tagged trail-ref | Each ref is N/S/E/W typed |
| Annotations on backlinks | **MCP-fed annotations** | Your annotations land in `vault/Human/{date}/*.md` AND POST to the faerie MCP server. On the next AI session, your annotations appear in the agent's system prompt as `human_annotations` — first-class steering signals. |
| Adjacent-panel peek (`CMD+Hover+Click`) | Same | Unchanged — peek is good. |
| Highlights | Bearing-colored highlights | Each highlight inherits its bearing's color in the visual language palette. |
| Collapse/expand (`Shift+Click`, `CMD+Shift+S`) | Same | Unchanged. |
| Source preservation | Forensic chain integrity | Every trail-ref records a sha256 of the source manifest. If the source mutates, the ref shows a ⚠ broken-chain glyph. |

### What's new beyond Latticework

- **Bearings as first-class typing.** Latticework's refs are untyped; ours carry compass semantics.
- **MCP feedback loop.** Your annotations don't just sit there — they reach the AI.
- **Compass overlay.** Juggl rendering shows the *typed* graph, not just the citation network.
- **Honeycomb canvas auto-arrangement.** The spatial layer is auto-populated with the day's work, then yours-to-edit.
- **Visual language consistency.** Every shape, color, and motion ties back to `forensics/glossary/VISUAL-LANGUAGE.md` — the apiary metaphor running through everything.

## Quick start (5-minute path)

1. **Install hive plugin** in your vault (`.obsidian/plugins/hive/`)
2. **Run `faerie: doctor`** from command palette — check dependency plugins (Dataview, Meta Bind, QuickAdd, Breadcrumbs, Juggl, Linter) are installed
3. **Run `faerie: install canonical configs`** — drops linter + Breadcrumbs settings, faerie CSS snippets, basic templates
4. **Set MCP token** — Settings → Hive → paste your bearer token from https://faerie.retrofuture.tech (GitHub OAuth)
5. **Open `00-SHARED/Dashboards/00-Home.md`** — your front page should now load on every vault open
6. **Open today's Daily folder** — find `{date}-board.canvas`, double-click — auto-arranged honeycomb of today's AI work
7. **Highlight any passage with `CMD+Shift+H`** — pick a bearing — see the colored ref appear
8. **Open "Faerie Live" side panel** — Watch live dashboard, metrics, charters from the MCP server
9. **Try `faerie: compass overlay for current note`** — Juggl view of your typed neighborhood

## Common workflows

### Authoring a new charter (intent declaration)

1. Command palette → "New charter (QuickAdd)"
2. Wizard prompts: slot1 verb (unblock/ship/extend/...), slot2 substrate (chat/condenser/...), slot3 domain (server/spa/...), optional texture
3. Basic template fills frontmatter shell; blueprint applies body sections (intent, deliverables, acceptance ritual, breadcrumbs)
4. Charter lands in `forensics/charters/{date}/{charter_id}.md`
5. Mirrored to vault `Daily/{date}/{charter_id}.md` on next mirror tick

### Following a trail end-to-end

1. Open `00-SHARED/Dashboards/01-Today.md`
2. Find a manifest in today's list; click through
3. Read the trail block (rendered by the daily-mirror blueprint)
4. `CMD+Hover+Click` on any trail waypoint → peek the referenced state without losing your place
5. Want to dive deeper into a step? `CMD+click` opens it as the active note
6. Compass overlay shows you's near in the typed graph

### Annotating to steer the next AI run

1. Read an AI-authored manifest
2. Find a passage that concerns you — `CMD+Shift+H`, pick W (baseline question)
3. The selection becomes a W-tagged ref with amber styling
4. Click on the backlink in the bottom pane → add a annotation: "this assumption needs re-validation, the data from yesterday contradicts it"
5. Annotations auto-sync to `vault/Human/{date}/a-{ts}.md`
6. Plugin POSTs to `faerie_record_annotation` on the MCP server
7. Next conversation: agent's system prompt includes your annotations in `{{ human_annotations }}`
8. Agent's first response acknowledges your concern and shifts approach

### Spatial brainstorming with real data

1. Open today's `{date}-board.canvas`
2. AI-arranged cards are honeycomb-tiled
3. Drag cards to cluster them differently — your spatial reasoning is now visible
4. Add a free-text card with your hypothesis
5. Draw an arrow from your card to an AI-authored manifest — typed by bearing if you want
6. Embed an Excalidraw sketch via `![[my-sketch.excalidraw]]`
7. Save — your spatial layer persists across mirror regenerations

## The point (restated)

You are not the AI's reader. You are the AI's **collaborator**. The hive plugin gives you the visual, spatial, and intertextual surface to engage that collaboration *non-destructively* — the forensic chain stays pristine; your annotations live in parallel layers that are themselves first-class inputs to the next round.

**Visual** — see the compass topology, bearings as colors, hex cells as work, parchment as intent, anchors as principles.

**Spatial** — arrange the day's work how it makes sense to *you*; add your own thinking next to the AI's; save the layout.

**Intertextual** — connect any passage to any other passage with semantic direction; let those connections become the substrate for the next conversation.

Build on Latticework's discovery that annotation and editing should live in one substrate. Add bearings, MCP feedback, honeycomb canvas, and the apiary metaphor. The result is a vault that's not a record of work — it's the **medium** of work.
