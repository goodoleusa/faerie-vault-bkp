## Critical Reframe (The Key Insight)

**From:** Droplets are interesting insights we capture to preserve signal.  
**To:** Droplets are Aha moments meant to inspire other agents. Write the spark BEFORE reasoning filters it.

**Why this matters:** Pre-reasoning sparks inspire; post-reasoning conclusions just inform. The unfiltered moment is the cross-pollination vector.
