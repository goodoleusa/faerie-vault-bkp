# Canonical Emergence Formulas

> *Opening summary: The canonical measurement formulas for emergence quality in the faerie system: Q (emergence quality, ratio of self-organized completions to total), R (routine-completion ratio), M (mission-graph completion ratio), F (free-choice ratio). These are the formulas the whitepaper §4.2 references when discussing shapes as the mutation-discipline measurement substrate. The threshold values Q≥0.7, R≥0.7, M≥0.7, F≥0.5 define the band where the system is considered emergent vs. orchestrator-dependent.*
