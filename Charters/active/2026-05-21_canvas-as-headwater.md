## Thesis

The BrainstormCanvas is where dreams become reality and flow into the rest of the collaborative dev
platform — the canvas is the headwater, not a feature beside it. Every primitive (charters, agents,
manifests, COC, vault publications, timeline events) is downstream of canvas intention AND back-
flows into the canvas as live state. Same fractal pattern at every depth: a card on a canvas can
itself open a canvas, recursively.
